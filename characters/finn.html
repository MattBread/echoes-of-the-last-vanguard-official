FINN CHARACTER ART

Finn

New Recruit · Vanguard

Age: 18

Character Overview

Finn is the youngest member of the central Vanguard team. Nervous, clumsy and often louder than the situation requires, he is still determined to prove that he belongs.

He makes mistakes, talks before thinking and occasionally knocks things over at exactly the wrong moment. But when someone is in danger, Finn can find courage he didn't know he had.

Personality

Finn is enthusiastic, awkward and easily flustered. He often shouts when nervous, sometimes raising his voice even when everyone is standing right beside him.

His clumsiness provides moments of humour, but it never means he is useless. Every so often, he surprises the team with an unexpectedly clever observation.

More than anything, Finn wants to prove that he can protect people instead of being someone who needs protecting.

Magic: Radiant Magic

Radiant Magic controls light, illumination and concentrated energy. Finn's Grimoire responds strongly to his emotions, making his magic powerful but sometimes unpredictable.

Grimoire: The Book of the First Dawn

Known Abilities

  • Flashpoint: Releases a sudden burst of blinding light.
  • Radiant Edge: Forms a concentrated blade of light.
  • Daybreak: Illuminates a wide area with powerful radiant energy.
  • Falling Star: Launches a concentrated blast of light toward a target.

The Cost of Radiant Magic

Finn's spells can become unstable when his emotions overwhelm his concentration. Overusing Radiant Magic can temporarily damage his eyesight, leaving him struggling to see.

The Boy Who Couldn't Save Anyone

Finn grew up in a small settlement that was attacked by a supernatural entity. During the evacuation, he became separated from his family.

While searching the ruins for survivors, he discovered a mysterious Grimoire. Its pages responded to his fear, unleashing a burst of Radiant Magic that saved several people.

But he couldn't save everyone. The experience left him terrified of causing another person's death. He joined the Vanguard because he never wanted to feel helpless again.

A Typical Finn Moment

"EVERYBODY GET DOWN! I THINK I KNOW WHAT I'M DOING!"

A loud crash follows. Finn insists everything is under control.
