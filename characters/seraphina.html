SERAPHINA CHARACTER ART

Seraphina

Researcher · Vanguard

Age: 34

Character Overview

Seraphina is a brilliant researcher devoted to understanding Grimoires, ancient inscriptions and the hidden laws behind the Written Arts.

Her knowledge is extraordinary, but her investigations often lead her into subjects that the Vanguard would rather keep buried. She is driven by questions that have followed her since childhood.

Personality

Intelligent, obsessive and intensely curious, Seraphina can become so absorbed in a discovery that she forgets everything happening around her.

She is mysterious and sometimes socially awkward. Her curiosity can make her appear detached, but she genuinely wants to understand the world and the people in it.

Magic: Forbidden Script Magic

Forbidden Script Magic allows Seraphina to manipulate inscriptions, magical laws and sealed knowledge. It is among the most dangerous branches of the Written Arts because it can interfere with the rules that govern other magic.

Grimoire: The Book of Unwritten Names

Known Abilities

  • Null Script: Suppresses or disrupts an active inscription.
  • Rewrite: Alters the structure of an existing magical inscription.
  • Name of Binding: Uses a true name to restrict a target.
  • Forbidden Chapter: Opens access to dangerous knowledge sealed within her Grimoire.

The Cost of Forbidden Magic

Forbidden spells demand a permanent price. Each use can take a memory from Seraphina, removing pieces of her own past in exchange for knowledge and power.

The more she learns, the less certain she becomes of what she can still remember.

The Forgotten Childhood

Seraphina grew up inside an institution dedicated to studying ancient Grimoires. Her childhood memories are fragmented, with entire periods of her life missing.

She suspects experiments were performed on her, although she has never uncovered the full truth.

Her greatest discovery is also her most disturbing: some of the writing found inside ancient Grimoires appears to be in her own handwriting, despite the books being centuries old.

"A book doesn't have to be alive to remember who opened it. Does it?"
