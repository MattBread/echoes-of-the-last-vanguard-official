CHARACTER OVERVIEW

Matt is a compassionate, observant and quietly humorous young man who joins the Vanguard searching for answers about his missing younger sister, Mara.

Years earlier, Mara disappeared at the age of seventeen during an evacuation of their residential district following a series of mysterious disappearances. No body was ever recovered.

The Vanguard dismissed the incident as an unfortunate consequence of the Collapse. Matt never believed them.

During his first deployment, a voice comes through a military radio. A voice he recognises immediately.

"Matt... you took too long."

PERSONALITY

Kind, empathetic, courageous and observant, Matt genuinely cares about the people around him. He often notices details that others overlook and has a quiet sense of humour.

He is not naturally interested in power or authority. His determination comes from wanting to protect people and uncover the truth.

As the story progresses, his connection to Echo Magic begins to undermine his confidence in his own memories and his understanding of reality.

MAGIC — ECHO MAGIC

Echo Magic allows Matt to perceive and interact with remnants of memories, sounds and events that have left an imprint on the world.

Echo Sight

Reveals traces of past events hidden within locations and objects.

Phantom Strike

Channels Echo energy through his Japanese katana, allowing him to strike supernatural entities.

Memory Trace

Allows Matt to experience fragments of memories connected to people or places.

Silent Corridor

Creates a temporary space where sound, perception and movement become distorted.

Requiem

A powerful Echo technique that releases accumulated Echo energy in a devastating attack.

PERSONAL GRIMOIRE

The Book of Forgotten Voices

A mysterious living fragment connected to forgotten memories and voices from the past. Its pages seem to recognise Matt, and it holds secrets that may reach far beyond his own lifetime.

THE COST OF POWER

Every use of Echo Magic places a strain on Matt's mind.

Repeated casting gradually erodes the reliability of his own memories. He may forget familiar faces, confuse memories with reality, or hear voices that nobody else can hear.

The more he searches the past, the less certain he becomes of his own identity.

PARTNERSHIP — CELESTE

Matt and Celeste are exceptionally close friends, trusted combat partners and co-leaders of their central team.

Matt's Echo Magic allows him to perceive things others cannot, while Celeste's Soul Magic provides powerful protection and close-range combat abilities.

Together, they possess a devastating combined technique known as Eclipse of the Fallen.

Their partnership is built on loyalty, trust and an unbreakable friendship.
