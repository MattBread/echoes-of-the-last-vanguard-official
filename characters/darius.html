DARIUS CHARACTER ART

Darius

Intelligence Officer · Vanguard

Age: 32

Character Overview

Darius is a Vanguard Intelligence Officer specialising in information gathering, surveillance and the investigation of unusual supernatural activity.

Charming and observant, he has a talent for noticing details others overlook. He rarely reveals everything he knows, making it difficult to tell where his loyalties truly lie.

Personality

Darius is intelligent, secretive and quietly charismatic. He often uses humour and conversation to put people at ease while carefully studying their reactions.

Beneath his confident exterior is a man afraid that his own identity may not be what he believes it to be.

Magic: Veil Magic

Veil Magic manipulates shadows, concealment, illusions and perception. Darius uses it to hide himself, misdirect enemies and create convincing distortions of reality.

Grimoire: The Book Behind the Mirror

Known Abilities

  • Shadow Walk: Moves through connected areas of shadow.
  • False Image: Creates convincing visual illusions.
  • Blackout: Conceals an area in supernatural darkness.
  • Mirror Prison: Traps a target inside a distorted illusion.

The Cost of Veil Magic

The longer Darius maintains an illusion, the harder it becomes to distinguish reality from his own creations.

Extended use can leave him questioning what he has actually seen, heard or experienced.

The Man Without a Past

Darius once had a family and an ordinary life. Following a mysterious incident, the people who knew him began remembering him differently.

Official records contradict his memories, and the few details he can verify seem to belong to different people.

He studied Veil Magic to understand the relationship between perception and reality.

His greatest fear is that the identity he is trying to recover may never have been real.

"The truth is easy to hide. The difficult part is remembering which version you told yourself."
