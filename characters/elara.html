ELARA CHARACTER ART

Elara

Medic · Vanguard

Age: 30

Character Overview

Elara is the team's medic, a highly intelligent and compassionate woman who has dedicated her life to understanding the human body and the strange forces that threaten it.

She is determined to save lives, even when the odds are against her. Her medical knowledge and magical abilities make her essential to the team's survival.

Personality

Intelligent, caring and occasionally sarcastic, Elara balances compassion with a practical understanding of danger. She can be warm and reassuring, but she has little patience for reckless decisions that put lives at risk.

She carries the emotional weight of every person she has been unable to save, even when she refuses to show it.

Magic: Vital Magic

Vital Magic manipulates biological energy and the body's natural processes. Elara uses it to heal injuries, stabilise patients and influence living systems.

Grimoire: The Book of the Living Vein

Known Abilities

  • Mending Touch: Accelerates the healing of injuries.
  • Blood Thread: Manipulates blood flow for medical or defensive purposes.
  • Second Pulse: Restores vital activity in a critically weakened patient.
  • Life Reversal: A dangerous advanced technique that can reverse severe biological damage.

The Cost of Vital Magic

Healing is never free. Elara absorbs part of the physical burden of the injuries she treats. Serious wounds can leave her exhausted or injured herself.

Vital Magic cannot casually bring the dead back to life. Even Elara's greatest techniques have strict limits.

The Unanswered Prayer

Elara grew up during an outbreak that devastated her settlement. She watched people die from a supernatural sickness that nobody understood and nobody knew how to treat.

She devoted herself to medicine and Vital Magic so that others would not have to suffer the same fate.

Behind the scenes, Elara is researching a way to reverse magical corruption. Some of her earlier experiments have ended in tragedy, but she has not abandoned the search.

"I can't promise everyone will survive. But I can promise I won't stop trying."
