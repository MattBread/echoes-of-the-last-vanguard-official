RIVEN CHARACTER ART

Riven

Rupture Specialist · Vanguard

Age: 26

Character Overview

Riven is an exceptionally skilled fighter whose confidence can sometimes come across as arrogance. Competitive and fiercely independent, he measures himself against everyone around him, especially Matt.

He believes strength is the only reliable way to prevent loss. Beneath his sharp tongue and difficult attitude, however, is someone who still carries the pain of a personal failure.

Personality

Arrogant, determined and intensely competitive, Riven rarely backs down from a challenge. He has little patience for hesitation and does not easily trust other people.

Despite his attitude, he is capable of genuine kindness. He simply finds it difficult to show vulnerability or admit when he needs help.

Magic: Rupture Magic

Rupture Magic manipulates pressure, force and fractures in space. Riven uses it to create devastating attacks, disrupt enemy movement and cross short distances through spatial distortions.

Grimoire: The Book of Broken Space

Known Abilities

  • Rift Step: Moves through a short spatial fracture.
  • Crimson Break: Releases concentrated destructive force.
  • Shatter Point: Creates a focused rupture at a chosen point.
  • World Splitter: A devastating high-level spatial attack.

The Cost of Rupture Magic

The pressure created by Riven's spells can damage his own body. Powerful techniques place enormous strain on his muscles, bones and internal systems. His greatest attacks can leave him barely able to stand.

The Second Place

Riven grew up constantly compared with an exceptionally gifted older brother. No matter what Riven achieved, he was made to feel that he was second best.

His brother disappeared during a supernatural incident while saving Riven's life. Since then, Riven has become obsessed with becoming strong enough that he will never lose someone that way again.

He secretly believes his own actions were responsible for his brother's disappearance.

"If you're not strong enough to protect what matters, what good is all that power?"
