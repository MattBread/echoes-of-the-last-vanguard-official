GARRICK CHARACTER ART

Garrick

Veteran · Vanguard

Age: 45

Character Overview

Garrick is a seasoned Vanguard veteran who has survived battles and horrors that would have broken most soldiers. Battle-scarred and experienced, he has learned to trust preparation over promises.

He is not easily impressed, but his experience makes him one of the team's most valuable members. Beneath his intimidating presence is someone who understands the cost of survival.

Personality

Quiet, intimidating and practical, Garrick rarely wastes words. His dry humour often appears at the worst possible moments.

He watches over younger soldiers without making a show of it. He has seen enough loss to know that surviving a mission is sometimes more important than winning it.

Magic: Ashen Magic

Ashen Magic controls ash, heat, combustion and the remnants of destroyed matter. Garrick uses it to obscure battlefields, create devastating attacks and turn the remains of destruction into weapons.

Grimoire: The Book of the Last Ember

Known Abilities

  • Ash Veil: Fills an area with ash to conceal movement.
  • Ember Fang: Forms a concentrated attack of burning energy.
  • Cinder Rain: Rains burning fragments over an area.
  • Ashes of War: A powerful advanced technique that unleashes destructive Ashen energy.

The Cost of Ashen Magic

Garrick's magic consumes oxygen and places enormous strain on his body. Prolonged use can leave him struggling to breathe and unable to continue fighting.

The Burning Settlement

Garrick survived the destruction of his hometown. He remembers the burning buildings, the smoke-filled streets and the people trapped inside.

He studied Ashen Magic to turn the destruction that took everything from him into something he could control.

Garrick recognises the creature responsible for the destruction. What troubles him more is the knowledge that the creature was not acting alone.

"I've seen what happens when people think they're ready for the dark. Stay close, keep your eyes open, and don't get careless."
