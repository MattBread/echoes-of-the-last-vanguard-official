Nova CHARACTER ART

Nova

Soul Binder · Vanguard

Age: 28

Character Overview

Nova is courageous, confident, fiercely loyal and deeply protective of the people she cares about. She combines an elegant presence with the instincts of an experienced fighter.

Her long blonde hair and regal appearance give her a distinctive presence, but she remains grounded, practical and ready to fight alongside her team.

Personality

Witty, compassionate and fearless, Nova is often the person who keeps others moving when everything seems lost. She can be fiercely protective, but she also knows when to challenge her friends.

Matt is her closest friend and trusted combat partner. They lead their central team together, sharing responsibility and relying on one another in battle.

Magic: Soul Magic

Nova's Soul Magic draws upon life force and spiritual energy. Through her Grimoire, she can protect allies, bind supernatural entities and channel energy into powerful attacks.

Grimoire: The Book of the Bound Soul

Known Abilities

  • Soul Shield: Creates a protective barrier around herself or others.
  • Binding Chains: Restrains supernatural entities with spiritual chains.
  • Vitality Transfer: Transfers life energy to an injured ally.
  • Spirit Blade: Channels Soul Magic through her weapon.
  • Sanctuary: Creates a protected area that shields those within it.

The Cost of Soul Magic

Soul Magic consumes Nova's own life energy. The more she protects others, the more physically exhausted and vulnerable she becomes. Even her strongest abilities carry a personal cost.

Combat Partnership

Nova and Matt fight as partners and lead their team together. Matt's Echo Magic allows him to sense traces of memories and events, while Nova's Soul Magic provides protection and powerful close-range attacks.

Their combined technique, Eclipse of the Fallen, joins Echo energy with Soul Magic to create an attack capable of damaging entities that ordinary weapons cannot defeat.

Both must remain connected and focused. If their magic loses balance, the technique can turn against them.

"If we're going to make it through this, we're making it through together."
