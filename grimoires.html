The Nine Known Grimoires

Each book is bound to its bearer. Each contains unique abilities, hidden knowledge, and a price that must eventually be paid.

ARCHIVE 001

Matt

THE ECHO BEARER
Echo Magic
The Book of Forgotten Voices

A mysterious Grimoire connected to memories, echoes, and voices left behind by the past. Its connection to Matt appears to run deeper than anyone understands.

KNOWN ABILITIES

  • Echo Sight
  • Phantom Strike
  • Memory Trace
  • Silent Corridor
  • Requiem

THE COST

Repeated use gradually erodes Matt's ability to trust his own memories and distinguish past events from the present.
ARCHIVE 002

Nova

THE SOUL BINDER
Soul Magic
The Book of the Bound Soul

A powerful Grimoire governing spiritual bonds, protection, and the connection between life and soul. Its true potential remains largely unknown.

KNOWN ABILITIES

  • Soul Shield
  • Binding Chains
  • Vitality Transfer
  • Spirit Blade
  • Sanctuary

THE COST

Nova sacrifices her own life energy when protecting or sustaining others. Powerful techniques leave her physically exhausted.
ARCHIVE 003

Kael

THE COMMANDER
Dominion Magic
The Book of the Iron Covenant

A defensive Grimoire focused on territory, barriers, command, and the protection of those standing behind its bearer.

KNOWN ABILITIES

  • Iron Wall
  • Command Seal
  • Thousandfold Guard
  • Final Authority

THE COST

Maintaining powerful defenses forces Kael to absorb tremendous pressure and damage through his own body.
ARCHIVE 004

Riven

THE RIVAL
Rupture Magic
The Book of Broken Space

A dangerous Grimoire capable of manipulating pressure, force, and fractures in the space surrounding its bearer.

KNOWN ABILITIES

  • Rift Step
  • Crimson Break
  • Shatter Point
  • World Splitter

THE COST

The immense pressure created by Rupture Magic can damage Riven's body, especially when he pushes beyond his limits.
ARCHIVE 005

Elara

THE MEDIC
Vital Magic
The Book of the Living Vein

A Grimoire governing biological energy, healing, and the delicate balance between life and death.

KNOWN ABILITIES

  • Mending Touch
  • Blood Thread
  • Second Pulse
  • Life Reversal

THE COST

Healing transfers some of the physical burden to Elara. Restoring life is not something she can simply do without consequence.
ARCHIVE 006

Garrick

THE VETERAN
Ashen Magic
The Book of the Last Ember

An ancient Grimoire controlling ash, heat, combustion, and the remnants left behind after destruction.

KNOWN ABILITIES

  • Ash Veil
  • Ember Fang
  • Cinder Rain
  • Ashes of War

THE COST

Powerful techniques consume oxygen and place enormous physical strain on Garrick.
ARCHIVE 007

Finn

THE NEW RECRUIT
Radiant Magic
The Book of the First Dawn

A volatile Grimoire that channels light, illumination, and concentrated radiant energy. Its young bearer is still learning to control it.

KNOWN ABILITIES

  • Flashpoint
  • Radiant Edge
  • Daybreak
  • Falling Star

THE COST

Overuse can temporarily damage Finn's eyesight. His unstable magic makes every major spell a risk.
ARCHIVE 008

Seraphina

THE RESEARCHER
Forbidden Script Magic
The Book of Unwritten Names

A forbidden Grimoire capable of manipulating inscriptions, magical laws, and knowledge that was meant to remain sealed.

KNOWN ABILITIES

  • Null Script
  • Rewrite
  • Name of Binding
  • Forbidden Chapter

THE COST

Forbidden spells permanently consume pieces of Seraphina's memory. Some knowledge cannot be recovered.
ARCHIVE 009

Darius

THE INTELLIGENCE OFFICER
Veil Magic
The Book Behind the Mirror

A mysterious Grimoire dealing with shadows, concealment, illusions, and the manipulation of perception.

KNOWN ABILITIES

  • Shadow Walk
  • False Image
  • Blackout
  • Mirror Prison

THE COST

Extended illusions can make Darius lose his ability to distinguish reality from what he has created.
