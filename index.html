THE BEGINNING

THE STORY

Ten years after the Collapse, humanity survives in fortified settlements scattered across a broken world. Abandoned cities conceal things that were never meant to be discovered.


The Vanguard protects the remaining survivors, investigating supernatural threats and recovering mysterious magical books known as Grimoires.


When Matt joins the Vanguard, searching for answers about his missing sister, he discovers a connection between his past, the Grimoires and something ancient that has begun to remember him.


Some memories should remain forgotten. Some voices should never answer.

THE WORLD AFTER THE COLLAPSE

A BROKEN WORLD

Ten years of survival. Thousands of unanswered questions.

The Collapse

A catastrophic event that shattered civilisation ten years before the beginning of the series. Its true cause remains unknown.

The Vanguard

A powerful military organisation protecting settlements, investigating disappearances and recovering dangerous Grimoires.

The Written Arts

An ancient form of magic requiring inscriptions, concentration and a price that can permanently change those who use it.

The Grimoires

Mysterious living fragments of an ancient intelligence. Every book holds its own consciousness, secrets and intentions.
