BOUND TO ITS BEARER: GARRICK

ISSUE ONE

THE ASHES REMAIN

Some fires destroy everything. Others leave behind something that refuses to die.

PANEL 01

The Veteran

Long before joining the Vanguard, Garrick learned what it meant to survive. Experience taught him that the worst things rarely announce themselves.

PANEL 02

A Town Reduced to Ash

His home was consumed by fire and an unknown creature. Garrick escaped, but the people and life he knew were lost.

PANEL 03 — THE DISCOVERY

Something in the Embers

Among the ruins, Garrick discovered an ancient Grimoire. Its pages carried the warmth of a fire that had supposedly burned out years before.

PANEL 04

The Written Arts

Garrick learned to command ash, heat, and the remnants of combustion. Every inscription brought the memory of his lost home closer.

PANEL 05

Not Alone

The creature that destroyed his hometown was not acting alone. Garrick knows something else was there, watching from beyond the flames.

THE WRITTEN ARTS

THE FOUR KNOWN SPELLS

Every spell must be inscribed. Every inscription demands concentration and a price.

SPELL 01

Ash Veil

Releases a dense cloud of ash that obscures vision and conceals Garrick and his allies from approaching threats.

SPELL 02

Ember Fang

Shapes burning ash into a concentrated blade of heat capable of cutting through obstacles and striking enemies.

SPELL 03

Cinder Rain

Scatters glowing fragments across an area, raining hot cinders down upon anything caught beneath them.

SPELL 04 — UNKNOWN LIMITS

Ashes of War

A devastating release of heat and ash. Its full inscription remains hidden, even from Garrick himself.

THE CONSEQUENCE

THE PRICE OF ASHEN MAGIC

EVERY FLAME CONSUMES SOMETHING

Ashen Magic draws upon the air around its bearer. The more Garrick commands heat and combustion, the more oxygen his body consumes.

Prolonged casting leaves him exhausted, struggling to breathe, and sometimes unable to stand. Push too far, and the fire he commands may become the last thing he ever sees.

RESTRICTED ARCHIVE

THE FIRE THAT NEVER ENDED

CLASSIFIED INSCRIPTION

Garrick remembers the night his hometown burned. He remembers the creature moving through the streets, and the silence that followed it.

But the Grimoire contains a fragment that suggests another presence stood beyond the destruction. Something that did not enter the flames. Something that watched.

"The fire was never alone."

— Unidentified inscription within the Grimoire
