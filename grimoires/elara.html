The Grimoire of Vital Magic

BOUND TO ITS BEARER: ELARA

ISSUE ONE

THE BURDEN OF LIFE

She learned how to save lives. Nobody taught her what saving them would take away.

PANEL 01

The Outbreak

Elara grows up during a devastating outbreak. As settlements collapse around her, she watches people suffer from an illness nobody understands.

PANEL 02

The First Healing

Desperate to save someone close to her, Elara discovers an ancient Grimoire. Its pages reveal a form of magic woven into the living body itself.

PANEL 03 — THE PRICE OF LIFE

"Take What You Need From Me."

Elara inscribes her first healing spell. The wounded person begins to recover, but the pain and exhaustion move into Elara. She realizes that her magic does not simply erase suffering. It transfers it.

PANEL 04

The Living Vein

She studies the Written Arts, learning to guide biological energy, repair damaged tissue and keep people alive when ordinary medicine fails.

PANEL 05

The Unanswered Question

Elara joins the Vanguard to investigate magical corruption and the strange illnesses spreading through the ruins. Some wounds resist even her strongest spells.

THE WRITTEN ARTS

THE FOUR KNOWN SPELLS

Vital Magic manipulates the energy of living things. Every act of healing demands a sacrifice.

SPELL 01

Mending Touch

Channels Vital Magic through Elara's hands to close wounds, repair damaged tissue and stabilize an injured person.

SPELL 02

Blood Thread

Creates delicate strands of magical energy that can bind wounds, connect damaged tissue and detect disturbances in a living body.

SPELL 03

Second Pulse

Forces a failing body to produce one more surge of vital energy, temporarily keeping someone alive when their body is close to shutting down.

SPELL 04 — FORBIDDEN LIMITS

Life Reversal

A dangerous technique that attempts to reverse catastrophic biological damage. Its true limits remain unknown, and it cannot casually restore the dead.

THE CONSEQUENCE

THE PRICE OF VITAL MAGIC

EVERY LIFE HAS A COST

Elara cannot create life from nothing. Healing transfers part of the burden to her own body. Severe injuries can leave her with pain, exhaustion, weakness and lasting damage.

The more she gives to keep someone alive, the less strength remains for herself. If she tries to reverse death, the consequences may be beyond anything she can survive.

RESTRICTED ARCHIVE

THE CORRUPTION IN THE BLOOD

CLASSIFIED INSCRIPTION

Elara's research has uncovered cases where the body appears to heal incorrectly. Wounds close, but something beneath the skin continues to change.

Some infected survivors carry traces of an energy that does not behave like ordinary Vital Magic. The Grimoire refuses to identify its source.

"Not everything that lives should be saved."

— Unidentified inscription discovered in the restricted pages
