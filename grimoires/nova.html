BOUND TO ITS BEARER: NOVA

ISSUE ONE

THE WEIGHT OF ANOTHER LIFE

Some people are born to survive. Others choose to make sure everyone else does.

PANEL 01

A Different Kind of Power

In a world where survival comes at a terrible price, Nova learns that protecting another person can demand more than courage.

PANEL 02

The Bound Book

An ancient Grimoire answers Nova's touch. Its pages carry inscriptions unlike anything she has ever studied.

PANEL 03 — THE FIRST BINDING

A Soul Reaches Out

When another life hangs in the balance, Nova writes her first inscription. Crimson and violet light surrounds her as a shield forms between death and the person she refuses to lose.

PANEL 04

The Strength to Protect

Nova discovers that Soul Magic can bind, defend, transfer vitality, and create a sanctuary for those under her protection.

PANEL 05

The Hidden Cost

Every life she protects takes something from her own. The Grimoire never promises that its bearer will emerge unharmed.

THE WRITTEN ARTS

THE FIVE KNOWN SPELLS

Nova's inscriptions connect the lives and energies of those around her.

SPELL 01

Soul Shield

Creates a protective barrier around Nova or another person, absorbing incoming harm through a bond of soul energy.

SPELL 02

Binding Chains

Summons spectral chains capable of restraining hostile entities and holding them in place.

SPELL 03

Vitality Transfer

Transfers life energy from Nova to another person, helping sustain someone who might otherwise perish.

SPELL 04

Spirit Blade

Forms a weapon of concentrated soul energy, allowing Nova to fight at close range with extraordinary precision.

SPELL 05 — THE SANCTUARY

Sanctuary

Creates a powerful protective field capable of sheltering multiple people. Its true limits and consequences remain unknown.

THE CONSEQUENCE

THE PRICE OF SOUL MAGIC

EVERY LIFE HAS A WEIGHT

Nova's magic consumes her own life energy. The greater the protection or transfer, the more deeply the cost is felt.

She can protect others, but she cannot give endlessly without consequence. Even the strongest soul has limits.

RESTRICTED ARCHIVE

THE SOUL BENEATH THE PAGES

CLASSIFIED INSCRIPTION

The Grimoire's oldest pages contain markings that do not appear in ordinary light. Some inscriptions seem to respond when Nova is near Matt.

There are things about Nova's connection to Soul Magic that even she does not fully understand.

One final inscription appears only briefly before vanishing again.

"A soul can carry more than one truth."

— Unidentified inscription within the Grimoire
