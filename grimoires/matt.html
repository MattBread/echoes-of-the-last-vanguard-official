ISSUE ONE

THE VOICE IN THE DARK

Every story begins somewhere. This one begins with a voice that should not exist.

PANEL 01

Ten Years After

The world has fallen silent. Settlements survive behind walls, and the Vanguard searches the ruins for answers.

PANEL 02

The Discovery

Deep beneath a forgotten structure, Matt discovers an ancient book. Its pages turn without a hand touching them.

PANEL 03 — THE FIRST WHISPER

"Matt... Can You Hear Me?"

A familiar voice escapes from the pages. A voice belonging to someone he has spent years trying to find. His missing sister, Mara.

PANEL 04

The Written Arts

Symbols appear across the pages. Matt realizes the book is not simply recording magic. It is teaching him how to write it.

PANEL 05

The Price

Each spell leaves something behind. The more Matt listens to the voices, the less certain he becomes of his own memories.

THE WRITTEN ARTS

THE FIVE KNOWN SPELLS

Every spell must be inscribed. Every inscription demands concentration and a price.

SPELL 01

Echo Sight

Allows Matt to perceive lingering traces of past events, revealing fragments of what happened in a location.

SPELL 02

Phantom Strike

Creates an echo of a previous movement, allowing a spectral attack to follow Matt's physical strike.

SPELL 03

Memory Trace

Lets Matt follow residual memories connected to a person, object, or place.

SPELL 04

Silent Corridor

Suppresses sound within a limited area, concealing movement and creating an unnerving silence.

SPELL 05 — UNKNOWN LIMITS

Requiem

A powerful Echo technique whose true capabilities remain unknown. Even the Grimoire seems reluctant to reveal its complete inscription.

THE CONSEQUENCE

THE PRICE OF ECHO MAGIC

MEMORIES CANNOT BE TRUSTED

Repeated use of Echo Magic slowly erodes Matt's confidence in his own memories. Faces become uncertain. Conversations change. Events he remembers clearly may never have happened.

The most terrifying possibility is that the voices in his Grimoire may know memories that Matt himself has forgotten.

RESTRICTED ARCHIVE

THE PAGES THAT SHOULD NOT EXIST

CLASSIFIED INSCRIPTION

Some pages appear blank until Matt is alone. Others contain handwriting that resembles his own.

There are references to events that have not happened yet. One page contains a name that Matt has not spoken in ten years.
