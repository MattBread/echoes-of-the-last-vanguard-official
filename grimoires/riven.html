BOUND TO ITS BEARER: RIVEN

ISSUE ONE

THE FRACTURE WITHIN

Some wounds never close. Some distances can never be crossed.

PANEL 01

The Last Escape

Years before joining the Vanguard, Riven and his older brother are caught in a disaster that leaves their home in ruins.

PANEL 02

The Sacrifice

With danger closing in, his brother stays behind to give Riven a chance to escape. Riven survives. His brother disappears.

PANEL 03 — THE FIRST FRACTURE

A World Torn Apart

Alone among the ruins, Riven discovers an ancient Grimoire. Its pages describe ways to split space itself. For the first time, the distance between him and his brother seems breakable.

PANEL 04

Rupture Magic

Riven learns to fracture the space around him, unleash destructive force and cross distances in the blink of an eye.

PANEL 05

The Obsession

Every new technique brings him closer to power, but farther from the person he used to be. Riven refuses to accept that his brother is gone.

THE WRITTEN ARTS

THE FOUR KNOWN SPELLS

Rupture Magic bends and breaks the space between things. Every inscription carries a physical cost.

SPELL 01

Rift Step

Allows Riven to tear open a brief spatial fracture and step through it, appearing a short distance away.

SPELL 02

Crimson Break

Releases a concentrated burst of rupture energy capable of smashing through obstacles and throwing enemies backward.

SPELL 03

Shatter Point

Creates a focused fracture at a chosen point, splitting solid surfaces or destabilizing an incoming attack.

SPELL 04 — UNKNOWN LIMITS

World Splitter

A devastating technique that tears open a massive spatial rupture. Its full range and consequences remain unknown, even to the Grimoire.

THE CONSEQUENCE

THE PRICE OF RUPTURE MAGIC

THE BODY BECOMES THE BREAKING POINT

Rupture Magic places tremendous pressure on Riven's body. Every fracture he creates sends a portion of that force back through him, damaging muscles, joints and bones.

The stronger the rupture, the greater the physical punishment. Pushing beyond his limits could leave him unable to move — or permanently destroy his body.

RESTRICTED ARCHIVE

THE BROTHER WHO VANISHED

CLASSIFIED INSCRIPTION

Riven has never accepted that his brother died. No body was recovered, and no reliable account explains what happened after the sacrifice.

Some pages in the Grimoire describe a presence trapped beyond a spatial fracture. The writing changes whenever Riven tries to read it.

"You were supposed to keep running."

— Unidentified inscription discovered within the Grimoire
