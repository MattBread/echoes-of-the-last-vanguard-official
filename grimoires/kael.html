THE IRON COVENANT

A Grimoire of Dominion Magic, written in the language of command, territory, and unbreakable defense.

Bearer: Kael

Classification: Dominion Magic

CHAPTER I — THE COMMANDER

The Man Who Holds the Line

Kael is a disciplined commander shaped by the battlefield. He believes that protecting others is a responsibility, not a privilege. Every decision carries the weight of the soldiers who trust him.

He once commanded a battalion that vanished during a disastrous retreat. The official report says they were lost. Kael has never accepted that answer.

CHAPTER II — KNOWN INSCRIPTIONS
01 — IRON WALL Creates a powerful defensive barrier capable of stopping incoming attacks and shielding those behind it.
02 — COMMAND SEAL Marks an area or target with a binding order, reinforcing Kael's control over the battlefield.
03 — THOUSANDFOLD GUARD Forms layers of overlapping defenses around allies. Each layer absorbs part of the incoming force.
04 — FINAL AUTHORITY Kael establishes an immense defensive territory, holding a position against overwhelming pressure.
CHAPTER III — THE PRICE OF DOMINION
Dominion Magic demands that Kael bear the force he prevents from reaching others. The wider and stronger his defenses become, the more damage and exhaustion his own body must endure. Even an unbroken wall can leave its keeper barely standing.
CHAPTER IV — THE LOST BATTALION

A Report That Was Never Closed

During a retreat, Kael was forced to abandon his position. His battalion disappeared, and the surviving records were sealed by Vanguard command.

One soldier may have survived. Somewhere beyond the known settlements, someone may still remember what really happened.
