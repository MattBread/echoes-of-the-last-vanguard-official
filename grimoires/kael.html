BOUND TO ITS BEARER: KAEL

ISSUE ONE

THE LAST COMMAND

A commander can give an order. But can he live with the ones he could not save?

PANEL 01

The Retreat

Ten years after the Collapse, Kael remembers the battlefield where his battalion was ordered to fall back. The horizon burned behind them.

PANEL 02

The Covenant

In the ruins of an abandoned command post, Kael discovers an iron-bound Grimoire. Its pages open to a command written in his own handwriting.

PANEL 03 — THE LINE HOLDS

Iron Wall

With his soldiers trapped behind him, Kael inscribes his first defensive spell. An immense barrier rises from the earth as something strikes from the darkness beyond it.

PANEL 04

The Missing

The official Vanguard report declares the battalion lost. But a broken transmission contains a voice that should have gone silent years ago.

PANEL 05

The Burden

Kael can shield an entire squad, but every impact against his defenses tears at his own body. A commander cannot protect everyone without paying a price.

THE WRITTEN ARTS

THE FOUR KNOWN SPELLS

Every spell must be inscribed. Every inscription demands concentration and a price.

SPELL 01

Iron Wall

Creates a powerful defensive barrier capable of stopping incoming attacks and shielding those behind Kael.

SPELL 02

Command Seal

Marks an area with a binding command, strengthening Kael's control over the battlefield and the ground he protects.

SPELL 03

Thousandfold Guard

Forms overlapping layers of defense around allies. Each layer absorbs part of an incoming force.

SPELL 04 — UNKNOWN LIMITS

Final Authority

Establishes a vast defensive territory, allowing Kael to hold a position against overwhelming pressure.

THE CONSEQUENCE

THE PRICE OF DOMINION MAGIC

THE WALL MUST BEAR THE WEIGHT

Kael's defenses absorb tremendous force, but that force does not simply disappear. The wider and stronger his barriers become, the more pressure and damage his own body must endure.

He can keep a line standing long after it should have fallen. The question is how much of himself will remain when the battle ends.

RESTRICTED ARCHIVE

THE BATTALION THAT NEVER RETURNED

CLASSIFIED INSCRIPTION

Vanguard records claim Kael's battalion was lost during a retreat. The surviving documents are incomplete, and the final transmission was sealed.

One soldier may have survived. Somewhere beyond the known settlements, someone may still know what happened that night.

"You left us behind."

— Unidentified voice recovered from a damaged military transmission
